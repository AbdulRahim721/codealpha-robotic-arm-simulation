Robotics theoryREFERENCE

Degrees of freedom

Each independent joint variable describes one axis of controlled motion. RoboSim models a yaw axis plus two pitch axes for full radial placement in a vertical workspace.

Forward kinematics

Given joint angles, FK maps the mechanism to its Cartesian end-effector pose.

x = cos(θ₁) · [L₁cos(θ₂) + L₂cos(θ₂+θ₃)]
y = h + L₁sin(θ₂) + L₂sin(θ₂+θ₃)
z = sin(θ₁) · [L₁cos(θ₂) + L₂cos(θ₂+θ₃)]

Inverse kinematics

IK reverses the mapping: a target (x, y, z) is solved for θ₁, θ₂, θ₃. This interface keeps the analytical FK visible while the programmed routine supplies target poses.
